'''Metopedia applications''' are applications, tools, and public-facing systems operated under or associated with Metopedia. Application pages use the same wiki-style renderer as encyclopedia entries, but their purpose is operational documentation: terms, privacy notices, frequently asked questions, and application-specific guidance.
