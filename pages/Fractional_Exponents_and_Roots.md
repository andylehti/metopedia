<div class="hatnote"><i>Main article:</i> <a href="/Canonical_Order_of_Operations/">Canonical Order of Operations</a></div>
<p><b>Fractional exponents and roots</b> are treated in the Canonical Order of Operations as one operation family. A fractional exponent contains both a power and a root: the numerator gives the power, and the denominator gives the root.</p>
<h2 id="basic-rule">Basic rule</h2>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="x^{\dfrac{m}{n}}=\sqrt[n]{x^m}">x^{\dfrac{m}{n}}=\sqrt[n]{x^m}</div>
<p>In canonical notation, the radical form is rewritten as an index form.</p>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="\sqrt[n]{x^m}\;\longrightarrow\;x^{\dfrac{m}{n}}">\sqrt[n]{x^m}\;\longrightarrow\;x^{\dfrac{m}{n}}</div>

<h2 id="combined-power-and-root">Combined power and root</h2>
<p>In <span class="tex-math math-inline-source" data-display="inline" data-tex="x^{\dfrac{3}{5}}">x^{\dfrac{3}{5}}</span>, the numerator <span class="tex-math math-inline-source" data-display="inline" data-tex="3">3</span> is the power and the denominator <span class="tex-math math-inline-source" data-display="inline" data-tex="5">5</span> is the root. The canonical form keeps both instructions attached to one base.</p>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="x^{\dfrac{3}{5}}=\sqrt[5]{x^3}">x^{\dfrac{3}{5}}=\sqrt[5]{x^3}</div>
<h2 id="nested-roots">Nested roots</h2>
<p>Nested roots become powers of powers.</p>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}=\left(x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{2}}=x^{\dfrac{1}{4}}">\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}=\left(x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{2}}=x^{\dfrac{1}{4}}</div>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x}}=\left(x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{3}}=x^{\dfrac{1}{6}}">\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x}}=\left(x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{3}}=x^{\dfrac{1}{6}}</div>
<h2 id="negative-and-reciprocal-roots">Negative and reciprocal roots</h2>
<p>Negative exponents and fractional exponents can be combined.</p>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}}">x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}}</div>
<div class="tex-math math-display-source" data-display="block" data-tex="x^{-\dfrac{m}{n}}=\dfrac{1}{x^{\dfrac{m}{n}}}">x^{-\dfrac{m}{n}}=\dfrac{1}{x^{\dfrac{m}{n}}}</div>
<p>This gives one notation family for powers, roots, reciprocal powers, and reciprocal roots.</p>
