<p>Use direct prose. Prefer definitional openings, stable sectioning, and restrained claims. Articles should read like reference entries rather than promotional pages.</p>
<h2 id="basic-article-pattern">Basic article pattern</h2>
<ol>
<li>lead section defining the topic</li>
<li>scope and context</li>
<li>key distinctions or subtopics</li>
<li>references where needed</li>
<li>categories at the end</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="house-preferences">House preferences</h2>
<ul>
<li>separate claims from examples</li>
<li>distinguish quotation from paraphrase</li>
<li>keep titles stable once linked widely</li>
<li>prefer explicit uncertainty over vague certainty</li>
</ul>
