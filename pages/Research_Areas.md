<p>Metopedia is organized around recurring fields of inquiry rather than a single disciplinary lane.</p>
<h2 id="cognitive-psychology-and-education">Cognitive psychology and education</h2>
<p>This area examines cognitive rigidity, bias recognition, belief preservation, conformity, educational conditioning, and failures of revision.</p>
<h2 id="textual-studies-and-linguistics">Textual studies and linguistics</h2>
<p>This area covers philology, textual transmission, translation disputes, semantic shift, historical language analysis, and the interpretation of difficult documents.</p>
<h2 id="mathematics-and-computational-logic">Mathematics and computational logic</h2>
<p>This area includes formal structure, notation, symbolic convention, order of operations, base systems, algorithmic reasoning, and computational tools.</p>
<h2 id="institutional-and-forensic-inquiry">Institutional and forensic inquiry</h2>
<p>This area addresses how institutions frame, preserve, defend, and sometimes insulate official narratives, with attention to records, methods, and evidentiary standards.</p>
