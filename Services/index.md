'''Metopedia services''' are applications, tools, and public-facing systems operated under or associated with Metopedia. Service pages use the same wiki-style renderer as encyclopedia entries, but their purpose is operational documentation: terms, privacy notices, frequently asked questions, and service-specific guidance.
