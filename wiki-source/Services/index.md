<div class="hatnote">This is a Metopedia project-index page. It organizes research areas, applications, forensic methods, mathematical frameworks, and related documentation.</div>

'''Research''' on Metopedia refers to the project space that gathers academic writing, applied tools, forensic methods, mathematical frameworks, cognitive models, and public application documentation. It is not limited to formal scholarship. It includes structured investigations, software systems, archived methods, policy pages, and explanatory material connected to Metopedia-associated work.

== Scope ==
The research index covers:

* '''Academic and educational analysis''' — writing on academic integrity, education systems, authorship, bias, and institutional procedure.
* '''Applications''' — operational tools and public software projects associated with Metopedia.
* '''Forensics and evidentiary methods''' — methods for evaluating images, documents, records, claims, and digital artifacts.
* '''Mathematical frameworks''' — proposed conventions, notation systems, computational mappings, and related examples.
* '''Cognitive psychology''' — models of rigidity, defensiveness, error, bias, and belief-preserving behavior.
* '''Textual and historical study''' — translation, manuscript interpretation, linguistic reconstruction, and source comparison.
* '''Institutional analysis''' — examination of systems, incentives, procedures, conformity, and inherited assumptions.
